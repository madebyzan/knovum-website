---
title: Organize a Space
description: Use sections, groups, tags and scenes so a large canvas always stays findable.
---

Once a Space fills up, what keeps it usable is structure, not memory. Knovum offers a few tools that don't overlap, each doing one job.

## Pick the right tool

| Tool            | Use it to                                                   | How                                                                            |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Section**     | Mark out a named area, such as “Evidence” or “To read”      | Press <kbd>S</kbd> to **Draw section**, or select items and **Create section** |
| **Group**       | Bind several objects so they move together                  | Select them and press <kbd>⌘/Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>G</kbd>                         |
| **Tag**         | Mark the same kind of content wherever it sits, then filter | **Add tag** in **Tags**                                                        |
| **Scene**       | Remember a viewport and return to it in one step            | **Save current scene** in **Scenes**                                           |
| **Portal node** | An entrance on the canvas to another Space                  | **New Portal node**                                                            |

Sections can be collapsed, or resized automatically with **Fit section to contents**; drag objects in or out to change what belongs to them. **Remove section only** deletes the section itself and leaves its contents in place.

## Arrange

- Select several objects and use **Align left**, **Horizontal center**, even spacing and more to line them up.
- **Auto layout (horizontal)** and **Auto layout (vertical)** arrange a set of items in one step.
- Lock anything you don't want to move by accident with <kbd>⌘/Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>L</kbd>.

## Find anything

- **Outline**: press <kbd>⌘/Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>O</kbd>. Click any item and the canvas moves to it, expanding its section if needed.
- **Find**: press <kbd>⌘/Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>F</kbd> to search the current Space.
- **Zoom**: <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>1</kbd> zooms to the selection, <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>2</kbd> to fit all content.
- **Focus**: select some items and choose **Focus selection** to show only them; press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to leave.

## Across Spaces

- Select items and use **Move to Space** to move them to another Space.
- **Favorite space** keeps a Space within reach under **Favorites** and **Recently opened** in the library.
- Each Space opens as a tab at the top. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Tab</kbd> switches tabs and <kbd>⌘/Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>W</kbd> closes one.

## Undo, archive and delete

Each Space keeps its last 100 edits. <kbd>⌘/Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd> undoes and <kbd>⌘/Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd> redoes.

For a Space you don't need right now, choose **Archive Space**: it leaves the library with its contents intact. In **Settings → Archived Spaces** you can **Restore Space** or **Delete permanently**. Permanent deletion can't be undone.

:::note[Deleting says the resource is in use]
While a document or video is open for editing in a tab, Knovum protects it from being deleted or moved to another Space. Close that tab, save or discard the changes when asked, then delete it.
:::
