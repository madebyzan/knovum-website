title: Structured knowledge
description: Make claims, evidence and unresolved questions explicit.
---

## Choose a meaningful object type

Structured Knowledge offers Concept, Question, Claim, Evidence, Experiment and Decision, plus custom object types. A Claim states something to evaluate; Evidence records why to believe or doubt it; a Decision records a choice. They are not interchangeable note colors.

## Connect reasoning

Use semantic relations such as supports, contradicts, depends-on, answers and derives-from. A visual line alone is not proof; its meaning and the content at both ends matter.

Keep a question open until its answer is justified. Link a claim to both supporting and contradicting evidence when the sources disagree. A derivation records a transformation; provenance describes origins. Do not conflate them.

## Review Knowledge Health

Knowledge Health helps locate unsupported claims, accepted claims with contradictions, evidence without origins, answered questions still marked open and stale provenance. Treat findings as prompts to inspect your reasoning, not automated truth judgments.

Relations and provenance are Space-local. Moving a source or changing its revision can affect references; inspect affected evidence rather than assuming every link remains current.
