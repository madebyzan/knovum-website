## Pick the source

Text nodes own reading and audiobook creation. Audio/video sources can provide a reference voice; video nodes own dubbing. Start from the relevant selected resource rather than creating an unrelated canvas object.

## Prepare an audiobook

1. Open the speech project and prepare the manuscript or chapter structure.
2. Choose an available voice revision for the relevant roles/paragraphs.
3. Adjust supported pauses, output format and metadata.
4. Audition representative paragraphs, including names and numbers.
5. Generate and monitor the durable Run.
6. Inspect the published audio and export the required deliverable.

Speech projects offer WAV, MP3 and M4B audio outputs. Saved settings and rendering are separate: changing text after an audition changes what must be rendered. Paragraph/chapter pauses are explicit; do not rely on silence accidentally left by a model.

## Understand voice identity

Voices are application-wide immutable revisions. Projects pin the selected revision, so changing a default does not change an old project. Voice creation requires a completed audition before saving. Reference clips are 5–30 seconds with adequate speech; confirmed transcripts must match the clip. Use only recordings you are entitled to use.

Optional VoxCPM2 cloning/design is hardware-intensive and requires at least 32 GiB physical memory. Delivery instructions are available only for compatible voice modes; they are not guaranteed emotion sliders.

Archiving a voice does not break existing pins. Permanent removal requires an archived, unreferenced profile. Space packages include referenced voices, not the whole global voice library or audition history.
