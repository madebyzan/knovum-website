## Start with a real question

Knovum brings sources, notes, structured knowledge and generated work into a local Space. Start with a question you actually need to answer, rather than importing your whole library at once.

1. [Install the right desktop build](./getting-started/installation/).
2. [Create your first Space](./getting-started/first-space/).
3. [Capture a few sources](./guides/capture/) and [organize them](./guides/organization/).
4. [Find relevant passages](./guides/search/), then [ask with citations](./guides/ask/).
5. [Export your work](./guides/export/).

## Choose the right tool

- **Find** retrieves existing material. Keyword search needs no AI connection.
- **Ask** produces an answer grounded in selected material; inspect every citation.
- **Create from selection** produces a new resource with a recorded relationship to its inputs.
- **Export** writes outside Knovum; it does not create another resource on the canvas.

## Local-first is a boundary, not a promise of no network

Your Space and its managed files are local. Website capture, model downloads and explicitly chosen AI providers can use the network. Read [privacy and AI boundaries](./reference/privacy/) before processing sensitive material.
