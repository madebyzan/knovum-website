---
title: "Meetings and lectures: from a recording to notes"
description: Record or import a meeting, turn it into notes, and trace every decision back to what was said.
---

This tutorial turns an hour-long meeting recording into notes organized by topic, with every decision linked to the moment it was made. Lecture and talk recordings work the same way.

:::note[You need]
A recording, or a meeting you are about to record. Transcription runs on your computer and needs no AI account; generating chapters and summaries and asking questions need an [AI provider](../../guides/providers/) or an enabled [desktop Agent](../../guides/agents/).
:::

## 1. Record or import

- Record live: in the project's Space, add an **Audio note** and start recording; choose **Finish recording** when you are done.
- Use an existing recording: drop the file onto the canvas, or choose **Import files…** in **Add content**.

Keeping the recording in the project's own Space puts the notes next to the rest of the project, so they turn up together in search later.

## 2. Transcribe

Select the recording, choose **Transcribe**, and follow the progress in the **Tasks** panel. When it finishes, the transcript is searchable like everything else in the Space.

If several people spoke, choose **Identify speakers** in **Speaker tracks** to see who said each line.

## 3. Get around quickly

Open the recording and click any line of the transcript; playback jumps there. When you want to double-check a moment, this is the fastest way: read to the spot, then listen.

## 4. Generate chapters and a summary

In the recording's **Media knowledge**, choose **Generate chapters & summary**. Knovum splits the transcript into chapters and writes a summary of each. Choose **Jump to chapter** on any chapter to return to that point in the recording. The shape of the meeting becomes clear at a glance.

## 5. Ask what was decided

Open the **Conversation**, choose an Agent and add the recording as a resource. To have the Agent work in a notes format, pick the bundled **Meeting notes** plugin in **Conversation extensions**. Then ask:

> What did this meeting decide, and who owns each decision?
> Quote the words where each decision was made, and list open questions separately.

Choose **Locate at source** for each one and listen to confirm the decision and its owner. Meetings are full of proposals that get overturned later; the recording is what counts.

## 6. Write the notes

Create a Markdown document with a heading for each topic, and under it the confirmed decisions and owners.

For decisions you need to follow up on, add a **Decision** object next to the recording and connect the words that made it with **Extract as evidence**. Anyone who opens that decision later can hear the original moment directly.

## 7. Find it again later

Weeks later, press <kbd>⌘/Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>F</kbd> in the Space and search for a name or a topic; if you don't remember the Space, press <kbd>⌘/Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>F</kbd> to **Search all Spaces**. Results take you back to that moment in the transcript and the recording.

## What you end up with

- A searchable transcript you can replay to the second, split into chapters.
- Notes by topic, with decisions and owners confirmed against the recording.
- Decision records that play the original moment in one click.
